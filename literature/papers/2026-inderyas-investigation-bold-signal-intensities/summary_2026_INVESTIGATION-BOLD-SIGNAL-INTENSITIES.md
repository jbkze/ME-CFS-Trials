## Summary

Using an extra-powerful (7 Tesla) brain scanner, researchers compared brain activity in 19 people with long COVID and 27 healthy people while they did a thinking task. The long-COVID patients had weaker brain-activity signals in the regions that handle attention and error-checking, and this was linked to slower thinking and more pain.

## Why this matters

Gives high-resolution evidence that long-COVID brain fog and fatigue come with real, measurable changes in brain activity, rather than just being tiredness — from a group that also studies ME/CFS brain imaging, strengthening the case for a shared biological basis.
