# Experimental hypoxia to probe neuro-metabolic and vascular dysregulation in ME/CFS: a multimodal proof-of-concept MRI study

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-bader-experimental-hypoxia-probe-neuro/citation.bib]]

## Summary

26 people with ME/CFS and 27 healthy people had brain scans (MRI) while breathing normal air and then air with less oxygen (a controlled, mild low-oxygen test). Patients' brains showed higher lactate (a byproduct of making energy without enough oxygen) even before oxygen was lowered, and their brain blood-flow response to low oxygen varied far more than in healthy people, alongside a slightly smaller brainstem.

## Why this matters

Gives direct brain-imaging evidence for the 'virtual hypoxia' idea in ME/CFS — that cells struggle to make energy properly and brain blood flow doesn't adjust normally under stress — and flags specific scan measures that could become an objective test for future trials.
