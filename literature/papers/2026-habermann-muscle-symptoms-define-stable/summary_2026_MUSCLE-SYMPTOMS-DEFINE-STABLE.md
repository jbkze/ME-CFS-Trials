## Summary

In survey data from the same 748 German ME/CFS patients studied before, the authors found that muscle-related symptoms (like muscle pain, weakness, and soreness after activity) form their own distinct, stable symptom group, separate from other symptom types — and that this muscle pattern differs somewhat between men and women, and around menopause.

## Why this matters

A third piece of evidence from this recurring German patient cohort that ME/CFS's muscle-related symptoms behave as their own biological cluster, and that sex and menopause status matter for how the illness shows up — relevant to designing sex-aware, muscle-focused future drug trials.
