## Summary

Using detailed eye-imaging technology, researchers found subtle changes in the small blood vessels and immune activity at the back of the eye in people with post-COVID syndrome, even when nothing looked obviously wrong on standard exams.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

The retina is one of the few places doctors can directly see small blood vessels and nerve tissue, so these subtle changes offer a non-invasive window into the vascular and immune problems that may underlie post-COVID and ME/CFS fatigue.
