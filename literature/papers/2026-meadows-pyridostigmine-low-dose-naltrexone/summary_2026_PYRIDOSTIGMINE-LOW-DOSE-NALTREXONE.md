# Pyridostigmine and low-dose naltrexone for ME/CFS: study protocol for the Life Improvement Trial (LIFT), a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial

## Summary

This is a study plan (a protocol, not results yet) for a US trial in 160 people with ME/CFS. Each person gets pyridostigmine (an old drug that boosts nerve signalling), low-dose naltrexone (an old drug used at a tiny dose), both, or dummy pills for 13 weeks, with exercise tests and daily-life measures to see what helps.

## Why this matters

Both drugs are already widely used off-label for ME/CFS without solid proof; this is one of the first careful tests of them alone and together in ME/CFS itself, so its results will matter — though this paper reports no outcomes yet.
