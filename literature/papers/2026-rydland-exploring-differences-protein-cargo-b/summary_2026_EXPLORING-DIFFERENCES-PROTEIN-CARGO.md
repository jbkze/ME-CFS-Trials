## Summary

Cells release tiny message-carrying particles into the blood called extracellular vesicles. Comparing blood from 49 ME/CFS patients and 50 healthy people, researchers found ME/CFS patients had more of these particles, and 11 of the proteins carried inside them were present in different amounts.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Adds to evidence that something measurable differs in ME/CFS patients' blood at the molecular level; these particles and their protein cargo are being explored as a possible future blood test (biomarker), though the small sample and modest-tier journal mean this is preliminary.
