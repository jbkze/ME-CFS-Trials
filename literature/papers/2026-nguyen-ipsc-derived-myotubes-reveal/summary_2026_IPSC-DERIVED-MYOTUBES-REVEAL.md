# iPSC-Derived Myotubes from ME/CFS Patients Reveal Signature Genes, Isoforms and Pathways with Drug Repurposing Potential

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-nguyen-ipsc-derived-myotubes-reveal/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Researchers turned blood-derived cells from 6 people with ME/CFS and 7 healthy people into lab-grown muscle cells and compared their gene activity. They found a few gene differences plus broader changes in how cells make proteins, handle damaged parts and use energy, and used this to suggest existing drugs that might be worth testing.

## Why this matters

Muscle problems may help explain the crashes after activity in ME/CFS, and a lab-grown muscle model gives a way to screen existing drugs without testing on patients. It is a small early study, so the drug ideas are only leads.

Walder group (Deakin/IMPACT, Australia); not Wirth/Scheibenbogen. Small n (6 vs 7); only 3 genes significant after FDR. Preprint, not peer-reviewed. Confirmed via Europe PMC record (PPR) on 2026-10-08.
