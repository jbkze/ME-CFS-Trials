## Summary

In 34 ME/CFS patients who had a lumbar puncture (a needle test measuring fluid pressure around the brain and spine), just over a third had higher-than-normal pressure — and those patients were far more likely to feel better in the days right after the procedure, which temporarily lowers that pressure.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Suggests a treatable fluid-pressure subgroup within ME/CFS, and that a simple pressure test could help identify who might benefit from pressure-lowering treatment.
