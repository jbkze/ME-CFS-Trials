## Summary

Using tiny stomach-lining biopsies from 12 long-COVID patients and 8 healthy people, researchers found physically fewer nerve fibres of the vagus nerve — a nerve that helps control digestion, heart rate, and blood pressure — in the patients.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

One of the first direct physical (not just functional) proofs of vagus-nerve damage in a post-viral illness, relevant to the dysautonomia (nervous-system control problems like dizziness on standing) seen in ME/CFS and post-COVID ME/CFS — though this is a very small study (12 patients) and needs replication.
