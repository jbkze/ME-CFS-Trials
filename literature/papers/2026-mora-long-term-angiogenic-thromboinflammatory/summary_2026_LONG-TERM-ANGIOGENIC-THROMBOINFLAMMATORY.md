# Long-term angiogenic and thromboinflammatory signatures in post-COVID-19 syndrome

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-mora-long-term-angiogenic-thromboinflammatory/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Researchers followed 32 people who had been hospitalized with COVID-19, three and a half years later. About two-thirds still had fatigue, breathlessness, or trouble concentrating, and their blood still showed high levels of proteins that drive blood-vessel growth (VEGF-D, angiopoietin-1) plus signs of a sluggish clotting system.

## Why this matters

Adds long-term evidence for a persistent blood-vessel and clotting abnormality behind lingering post-COVID fatigue — the same 'microclot'/vascular idea that also motivates some ME/CFS treatment approaches.

Published online Aug 7, 2026 (print vol. 29(4)). n=32 previously-hospitalized COVID-19 patients, 3.5-year follow-up; persistent symptoms (fatigue/dyspnea/concentration problems) in ~65%; elevated VEGF-D and angiopoietin-1, raised alpha-2-macroglobulin, prolonged prothrombin time.
