# A distinct effector B cell population drives autoantibody production in SARS-CoV-2 infection

## Summary

Studying blood samples from COVID-19 patients, researchers found a specific type of immune cell (a kind of B cell) that makes autoantibodies — antibodies that mistakenly attack the body's own tissues — and identified a signal that switches these cells on.

## Why this matters

Autoantibodies are a leading suspect behind symptoms in ME/CFS and long COVID; pinpointing the exact cell type and its trigger gives researchers a specific new target to aim future drugs at.
