## Summary

In this England-based trial, 78 adults with lasting brain fog after COVID-19 were randomly given either ten weekly one-on-one 'cognitive rehabilitation' sessions (practical coaching on personally chosen everyday goals, like remembering appointments) or usual care. People who got the coaching made significantly more progress on their chosen goals than those on usual care, and much of the benefit was still there six months later.

## Why this matters

A well-designed randomized trial — a rarer and stronger type of evidence — showing a real, non-drug treatment can meaningfully help the brain-fog side of long COVID, a symptom with no approved treatment.
