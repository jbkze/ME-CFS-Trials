# Australian registry reports poor health and wellbeing in people living with ME/CFS

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-eaton-australian-registry-reports-poor/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Using Australia's ME/CFS patient registry (2,873 patients compared with 797 healthy people), researchers found that quality of life is significantly worse in people with ME/CFS than in healthy people, and that patients' symptoms cluster into four distinct patterns rather than one uniform illness.

## Why this matters

One of the largest patient-registry studies of ME/CFS health burden to date; it reinforces the idea that ME/CFS is not one single illness but splits into distinguishable subgroups, which matters for designing better-targeted future clinical trials and for choosing which diagnostic checklist research should standardize on.
