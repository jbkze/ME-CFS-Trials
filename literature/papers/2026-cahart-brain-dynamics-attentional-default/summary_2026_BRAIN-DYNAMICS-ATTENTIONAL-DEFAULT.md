## Summary

Resting-state brain scans of 20 people with post-COVID syndrome and 20 healthy people found that patients' brains spent less time in an "alert, paying-attention" pattern and more time in an "inward-focused, emotional" pattern.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Suggests post-COVID fatigue and brain fog may involve the brain getting "stuck" in a less outward-focused mode — a possible objective brain signature that could one day help diagnose the condition or judge whether a treatment is working.
