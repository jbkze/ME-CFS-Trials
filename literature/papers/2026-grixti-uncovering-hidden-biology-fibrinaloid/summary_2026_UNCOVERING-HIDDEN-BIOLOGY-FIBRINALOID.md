## Summary

"Microclots" are unusually tough, hard-to-dissolve blood clots that some researchers think build up in inflammatory illnesses. This review by a leading microclot research group explains how these clots form, why the body struggles to break them down, and how they might cause symptoms in diseases including ME/CFS and long COVID.

## Why this matters

Consolidates and extends the microclot/endothelial (blood-vessel-lining) hypothesis that also motivates some ME/CFS and long-COVID treatment ideas, from the research group (Pretorius/Kell) most closely associated with this line of work.
