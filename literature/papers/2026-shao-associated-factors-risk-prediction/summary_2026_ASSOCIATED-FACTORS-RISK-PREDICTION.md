## Summary

In a Chinese hospital study of 610 people with chronic fatigue syndrome, almost half also had a circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorder — a mismatch between the body's internal clock and normal sleep and wake times. The researchers built a simple scoring tool using factors like shift work, how severe the fatigue is, being female, and reduced deep sleep to help predict who has this sleep disorder.

## Why this matters

Offers doctors a quick screening tool to spot which CFS patients also have a treatable sleep-timing problem, so sleep health could be addressed alongside other CFS care — though it comes from a single Chinese hospital and it's unclear whether standard international ME/CFS diagnostic criteria were used.

## Notes

Front Psychiatry, published 2026-07-20. PMID 42548707.
