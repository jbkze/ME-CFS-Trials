# Can Depression Polygenic Risk Score Predict Postinfective Fatigue Syndrome (PIFS) in the Dubbo Infection Outcomes Study (DIOS)?

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-ruseva-can-depression-polygenic-risk/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Researchers followed 464 people after a confirmed infection (like glandular fever) to see who went on to develop postinfective fatigue syndrome (a CFS-like illness after infection) six months later, and tested whether their inherited genetic risk for depression or for chronic fatigue syndrome could predict who would get sick.

## Why this matters

Neither genetic risk score predicted the illness overall, pushing back on the idea that post-infective ME/CFS-like illness is really just depression in disguise — though a weak, exploratory hint that depression genetic risk raises the odds in women but lowers them in men needs confirming in a bigger study.
