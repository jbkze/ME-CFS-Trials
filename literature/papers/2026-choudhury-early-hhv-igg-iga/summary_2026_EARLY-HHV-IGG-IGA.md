## Summary

Researchers measured antibodies against a very common dormant virus most people already carry (HHV-6, a herpesvirus) in blood drawn right when people first caught COVID-19. One antibody pattern (high 'IgA' antibody against HHV-6) at that early point was linked to a higher chance of later developing long COVID, while a different pattern ('IgG' antibody) was linked to a lower chance.

## Why this matters

Adds evidence that a dormant, usually-harmless virus 'waking up' around the time of the original infection may help set someone up for long COVID/ME-CFS-like illness, and hints at an early blood test that might one day flag who is at risk before symptoms even start.
