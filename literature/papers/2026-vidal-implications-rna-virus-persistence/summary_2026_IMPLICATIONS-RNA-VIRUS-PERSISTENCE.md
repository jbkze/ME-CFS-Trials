## Summary

A high-profile review argues that RNA viruses like SARS-CoV-2 can leave lingering traces in the body — leftover viral genetic material or proteins — long after the active infection is gone, and that this leftover material may keep driving inflammation in long-lasting post-infection illnesses.

## Why this matters

A top-journal endorsement of the 'lingering virus' theory behind ME/CFS and long COVID, relevant to the pathomechanism track even though it presents a synthesis of existing evidence rather than new experiments.
