# Facility-Measured Sleep Electroencephalographic Microstructures in Long COVID

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-sun-facility-measured-sleep-electroencephalographic/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Overnight brain-wave recordings (sleep EEG) compared 28 people with long COVID, 19 with ME/CFS, and 28 healthy people. Both patient groups showed unusual patterns in the fine details of their sleep brain waves, but ME/CFS patients had some extra abnormalities — like disconnected slow 'sleep spindles' — not seen in long COVID, suggesting the two illnesses disturb sleep in overlapping but not identical ways.

## Why this matters

Offers a measurable, objective marker for the poor, unrefreshing sleep that ME/CFS and long-COVID patients commonly report, and hints that ME/CFS may involve extra sleep disruption beyond what's seen in long COVID.
