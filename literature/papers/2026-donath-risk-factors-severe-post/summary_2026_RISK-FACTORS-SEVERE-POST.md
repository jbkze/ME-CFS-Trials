## Summary

In a German study of 120 young people (7–25 years old) with long-lasting COVID symptoms, having 12 or more symptoms during the original infection, plus dizziness or fainting-type symptoms (a sign of the body struggling to regulate blood flow when standing up), strongly predicted who would go on to develop the most severe, disabling form of the illness — 24% of whom were ultimately diagnosed with ME/CFS.

## Why this matters

Gives doctors an early-warning checklist for spotting which children are at risk of the most severe, ME/CFS-associated outcome after COVID, potentially enabling earlier intervention. Scheibenbogen is a co-author — HIGH PRIORITY.
