## Summary

A survey of over 9,600 New York City adults who previously had COVID-19 found that about one in five still gets post-exertional malaise (PEM) -- a crash after physical or mental effort -- and roughly 4% have symptoms lasting long enough to meet ME/CFS criteria.

## Why this matters

It's one of the largest population-representative estimates of how common PEM is after COVID-19, and shows it falls hardest on women, transgender/non-binary people, people of color, and people with less education or existing disabilities -- useful evidence for why clinics should screen for PEM routinely.
