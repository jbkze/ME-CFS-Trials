## Summary

Detailed immune-cell testing on blood from 103 ME/CFS patients, 63 long COVID patients and 41 healthy people found the two illnesses look different at the immune-cell level: long COVID showed signs of an immune system still fighting but wearing out, while ME/CFS showed a more suppressed, poorly-coordinated immune response.

## Why this matters

Even though ME/CFS and long COVID often look similar to patients, this suggests their underlying immune problems differ — meaning treatments effective for one may not automatically work for the other, an important consideration when designing future drug trials.
