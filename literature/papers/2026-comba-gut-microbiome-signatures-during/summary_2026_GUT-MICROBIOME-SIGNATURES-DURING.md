# Gut microbiome signatures during acute infection are associated with long COVID

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-comba-gut-microbiome-signatures-during/citation.bib]]

## Summary

In people followed from the time of their original COVID-19 infection onward, the mix of bacteria in their gut measured during that early, acute illness — not afterward — predicted who would go on to develop long COVID, and different bacterial patterns lined up with different symptom patterns.

## Why this matters

Suggests a gut-bacteria test taken during the initial infection could one day help flag who is at risk of long COVID, and points to the gut-immune connection as a possible early treatment target.
