## Summary

Tracking 102 post-COVID patients and 102 recovered people for six months, a simple hand-grip-strength test (measuring muscle force and how quickly it tires) stayed abnormal the whole time in patients, and a weaker grip at the start predicted worse fatigue and low mood six months later.

## Why this matters

Proposes a simple, objective, repeatable test — hand-grip strength — as a stand-in marker for muscle-related symptom severity in post-COVID illness, echoing the muscle-focused mechanism Wirth and Scheibenbogen propose for ME/CFS, though it is not from their group.
