## Summary

This mini-review looks at hydrogen-rich water — ordinary water with extra dissolved hydrogen gas — as a possible ME/CFS treatment. It calms harmful cell damage (antioxidant effect), reduces inflammation, and may help the cell's energy factories (mitochondria) work better. Three small early studies found it feasible and modestly helpful for fatigue, with mild side effects.

## Why this matters

A low-cost, low-risk supplement with early but weak evidence for reducing fatigue — worth watching, though the studies so far are small, not blinded, and rely on patients' own symptom reports rather than objective measurements.
