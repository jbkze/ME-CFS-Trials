## Summary

32 long-COVID patients in Japan were followed for 6 months. Two blood markers — one showing stress in the cell's 'power plants' (mitochondria) and one showing damage to the lining of blood vessels — stayed high in patients who hadn't gotten better, but dropped in those who improved. 91% of participants had fatigue as a main symptom.

## Why this matters

The authors directly link these findings to ME/CFS, arguing the same mitochondrial and blood-vessel problems seen here likely underlie ME/CFS fatigue too, and offer a simple blood test for tracking who stays stuck in prolonged illness.
