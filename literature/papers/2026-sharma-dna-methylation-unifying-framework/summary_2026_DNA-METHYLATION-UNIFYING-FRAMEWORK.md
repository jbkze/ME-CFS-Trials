## Summary

This review looks at DNA methylation (chemical tags that switch genes on or off) in ME/CFS, long COVID, fibromyalgia and hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome. These conditions share many symptoms, and the authors suggest they may share a biological root.

## Why this matters

It argues for studying these conditions together, which could lead to shared biomarkers (measurable biological signs) and treatment targets.
