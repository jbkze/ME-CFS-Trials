# Fibromyalgia, Chronic Fatigue Syndrome, and Multiple Sclerosis are associated with lower translocator protein binding affinity genotypes

## Summary

Researchers checked a gene variant that affects brain-scan dyes used to look for inflammation in the brain. People with ME/CFS, fibromyalgia or multiple sclerosis more often carried the variants that bind the dye less well than healthy people did.

## Why this matters

Many brain-inflammation scans depend on this gene, so results in ME/CFS could be skewed by it. The study is small and has not yet been peer-reviewed.

## Notes

Preprint; 21 ME/CFS participants, 86 total; genotype rs6971 (TSPO).
