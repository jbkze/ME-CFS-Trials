# Acute Protein Responses Control SARS-CoV-2-specific Neurocognitive and General Post-Viral Sequelae

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-liou-acute-protein-responses-control/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Researchers tested saliva samples taken soon after a COVID-19 infection and found that people whose bodies made more of certain antiviral immune proteins early on were less likely to develop lasting brain fog and fatigue-type symptoms months later.

## Why this matters

A simple, early saliva test might one day flag who is at risk of long-term post-viral fatigue and brain fog, and points to specific immune proteins as possible treatment targets.
