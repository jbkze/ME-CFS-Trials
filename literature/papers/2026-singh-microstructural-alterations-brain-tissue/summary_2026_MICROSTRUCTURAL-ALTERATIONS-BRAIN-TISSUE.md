## Summary

Using two advanced brain-scan techniques that map the tiny fibres connecting brain regions (diffusion tensor and diffusion kurtosis imaging), researchers compared brain structure in 37 people with ME/CFS, 19 with long COVID, and 27 healthy people. Both illnesses showed subtle differences from healthy brains, but in different areas and patterns — including changes in a motor-planning region and in the bundle of fibres linking the brain's two hemispheres — suggesting ME/CFS and long COVID are not simply the same condition at the brain-structure level.

## Why this matters

One of the first studies to directly compare brain wiring in ME/CFS versus long COVID rather than each against healthy people alone, adding evidence the two conditions may need to be studied and treated as biologically distinct rather than interchangeable.
