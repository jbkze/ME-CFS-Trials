## Summary

Using blood chemistry (metabolite) profiles from 197 people (106 with ME/CFS, 91 healthy), a machine-learning model could tell patients from healthy people apart with high accuracy — the strongest clues were an altered tryptophan pathway (the body's route for making mood-related brain chemicals) and signs of poor energy production in mitochondria (the cell's energy factories).

## Why this matters

Adds evidence toward a future blood test for ME/CFS and reinforces two recurring mechanistic themes in this database — mitochondrial energy failure and tryptophan-pathway disruption.

## Notes

Published Jun 30, 2026. 197 participants (106 ME/CFS, 91 controls); ensemble ML classifiers on 888 plasma metabolomic/lipidomic features reached 0.909 accuracy / 0.940 AUC; top discriminating interaction was proline & indole-3-lactate.
