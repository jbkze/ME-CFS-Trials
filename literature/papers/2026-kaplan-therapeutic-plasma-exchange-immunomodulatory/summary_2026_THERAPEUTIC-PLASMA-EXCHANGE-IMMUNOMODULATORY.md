## Summary

A review article argues that ME/CFS, long COVID, chronic Lyme disease, and certain childhood post-infectious conditions all involve the immune system misfiring in similar ways — including inflammation and self-attacking antibodies — and discusses whether blood-filtering treatments like plasma exchange could help.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Argues that past large trials of these therapies underperformed because they enrolled patients without using biomarkers to pick who was likely to benefit — a lesson for how future ME/CFS drug trials should select patients.
