# Exploratory clinical and immunological changes following Tixagevimab/Cilgavimab administration in immunodeficient patients with SARS-CoV-2-associated ME/CFS and persistent circulating spike protein

## Summary

Nine people with long-COVID ME/CFS and a weak immune system, who still had a piece of the coronavirus (spike protein) in their blood, were given an antibody drug that targets the virus. One type of autoantibody (antibodies that mistakenly attack the body's own receptors) went down, but spike levels and symptoms did not change clearly.

## Why this matters

It tests the idea that leftover virus pieces keep ME/CFS going. The result is weak and the study is tiny with no comparison group, so it is only a lead, not evidence the drug works.

## Notes

Babel group (Germany); not Wirth/Scheibenbogen. Small non-randomized observational study (n=9 paired). Confirmed via Europe PMC record (PMID 42843426) on 2026-10-17.
