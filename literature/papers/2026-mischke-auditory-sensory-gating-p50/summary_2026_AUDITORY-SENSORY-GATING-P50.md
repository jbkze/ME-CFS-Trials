# Auditory sensory gating (P50 suppression) as a biomarker of cognitive fatigue in long COVID

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-mischke-auditory-sensory-gating-p50/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Researchers measured a specific brain-wave response called P50 suppression — how well the brain filters out repeated, unimportant sounds — in 71 people with long-COVID fatigue. People whose brains were worse at this filtering task reported more mental (cognitive) fatigue, even after accounting for depression.

## Why this matters

Points to a simple, objective brain-wave measurement that tracks brain-based fatigue independently of mood, which could help build a much-needed objective test for 'brain fog' in long COVID and ME/CFS.
