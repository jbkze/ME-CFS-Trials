## Summary

Combining 8 trials with 715 patients, acupuncture reduced overall, physical, and mental fatigue more than control treatments, with no difference in side effects — though the authors themselves caution the trials were small and of uneven quality.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Adds acupuncture to the list of non-drug approaches with some trial evidence for ME/CFS fatigue, though the low-confidence evidence base (small, uneven-quality trials, flagged by the authors themselves) means it should not be over-weighted against biomedical treatment approaches.
