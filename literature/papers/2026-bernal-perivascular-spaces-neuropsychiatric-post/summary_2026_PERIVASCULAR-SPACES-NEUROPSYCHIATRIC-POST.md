# Perivascular spaces in neuropsychiatric post-COVID syndrome

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-bernal-perivascular-spaces-neuropsychiatric-post/citation.bib]]

## Summary

Brain scans of 116 people (59 with lasting post-COVID brain fog/fatigue, 57 without) looked at tiny fluid channels around brain blood vessels ('perivascular spaces', part of the brain's waste-clearance system). Older patients and those affected for longer had more enlarged channels, and this was linked to worse physical functioning.

## Why this matters

Adds a concrete, visible brain-imaging marker to the growing vascular/glymphatic (brain-fluid-drainage) evidence relevant to ME/CFS-type illness.
