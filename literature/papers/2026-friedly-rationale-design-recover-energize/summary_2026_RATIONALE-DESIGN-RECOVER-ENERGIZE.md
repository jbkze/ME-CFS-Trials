## Summary

This is a trial-design paper, not results yet: it lays out a large US 'platform trial' — a flexible design that can test several treatments over time within one shared structure — for exercise intolerance and post-exertional malaise in long COVID, run through the NIH's RECOVER initiative.

## Why this matters

A major, NIH-funded piece of trial infrastructure signalling a coming wave of rigorously tested treatments specifically for post-exertional malaise and exercise intolerance, an area with very few good trials so far; includes well-known ME/CFS exercise-physiology researchers (Lucinda Bateman, Harry Rossiter). Worth tracking for when the first results post.
