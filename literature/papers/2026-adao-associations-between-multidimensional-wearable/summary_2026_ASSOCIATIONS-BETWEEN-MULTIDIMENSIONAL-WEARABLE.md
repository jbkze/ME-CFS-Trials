## Summary

30 people in post-COVID rehab wore sensors day and night that tracked their heart rate and breathing. Physical tiredness, mental tiredness, and emotional tiredness each lined up with a different pattern in these body signals.

## Why this matters

Supports using wrist- or chest-worn sensors as an objective way to track different kinds of fatigue over time — useful for helping patients pace themselves and for measuring whether future treatments actually help.
