# Stress-inducible WASF3 regulates brain metabolism and cognitive function

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-syed-stress-inducible-wasf3-regulates/citation.bib]]

## Summary

In mice, a protein called WASF3, which is switched on by cell stress and is raised in ME/CFS, slowed the energy-making machinery of brain cells and worsened learning. Mice without it kept their memory better under stress.

## Why this matters

It offers a possible biological explanation for the 'brain fog' in ME/CFS and a protein that could one day be targeted, but it is mouse work and not yet tested in patients.
