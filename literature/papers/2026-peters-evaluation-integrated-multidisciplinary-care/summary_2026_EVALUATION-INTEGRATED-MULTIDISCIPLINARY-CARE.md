## Summary

The Charité Fatigue Center tested a structured care programme for ME/CFS — pacing advice, sleep and pain management, nutrition counselling, social support, and tailored inpatient rehab — against usual single-visit care, following 89 patients on the programme and 93 on usual care for a year.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

The extra structured care did not improve patients' physical functioning or other key outcomes after a year, and disability actually got worse for nearly half of the programme group — a disappointing but important negative result showing that good care coordination alone isn't a substitute for an effective medical treatment.
