## Summary

Spinal-fluid tests in 50 people with lingering post-COVID brain fog and fatigue, compared with 50 fully recovered people, found no extra brain immune activity or self-attacking antibodies in the affected group — even though their memory and fatigue were clearly worse.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

A notable negative finding arguing against ongoing brain inflammation or autoimmunity as the main driver of persistent post-COVID neurological symptoms, pointing research toward other explanations (such as the metabolic and vascular changes other new papers this run point to).
