## Summary

Blood was tested from 20 people with long COVID and 19 healthy people. The long-COVID patients' blood clotted faster and firmer than normal (called hypercoagulability), and contained more 'microclots' — tiny clumps of a misfolded blood protein that special dyes can detect sticking to them.

## Why this matters

Adds more detailed evidence for the microclot theory of long COVID and ME/CFS — the idea that abnormal, hard-to-dissolve clots clog small blood vessels and starve tissues of oxygen, from Etheresia Pretorius's group, the lab most associated with this research direction.
