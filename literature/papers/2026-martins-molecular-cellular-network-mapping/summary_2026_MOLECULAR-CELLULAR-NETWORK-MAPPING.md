## Summary

Brain scans of 20 people with ongoing tiredness after a mild COVID-19 infection showed subtle thickness changes in certain brain regions — especially areas rich in the same protein the coronavirus uses to enter cells, and areas involved in mood and the brain chemical serotonin.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Gives direct brain-imaging evidence that fatigue can follow even a mild infection, and links it to specific, testable biology (viral-entry receptors, serotonin circuits) that could point to future drug targets.
