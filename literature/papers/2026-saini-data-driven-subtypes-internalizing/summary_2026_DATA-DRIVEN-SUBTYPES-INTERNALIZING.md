## Summary

Using health-survey data from almost 73,000 adults in the Netherlands, researchers grouped people by their pattern of symptoms rather than by diagnosis label, looking at depression, anxiety, ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, and irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) together. One group, marked mainly by mental fog and fatigue, showed heavy overlap between ME/CFS-type symptoms and depression/anxiety symptoms, while pain-type symptoms formed their own separate group.

## Why this matters

Suggests some of the well-known overlap between ME/CFS and mood disorders may come from shared, non-specific symptoms (like fatigue and poor concentration) rather than the conditions truly being the same illness — relevant to the case for needing objective biomarkers to draw a clear line between them.
