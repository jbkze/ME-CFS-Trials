## Summary

Cells release tiny particles into the blood called extracellular vesicles (EVs), a bit like sealed messages. In long-COVID patients, these EVs carry unusual sugar coatings on their surface. Researchers show a plant-derived sugar-binding material can selectively grab EVs with this coating out of blood — a step toward both a diagnostic test and a way to filter disease-linked particles out of the blood.

## Why this matters

From the well-known UCSF LIINC long-COVID research group; opens up both a new biomarker idea and a possible future blood-filtering (apheresis-like) treatment approach, similar in spirit to the immunoadsorption trials already tracked here.
