# Regulatory Cycles of Orexin and Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 in Postviral Syndromes

## Related files

## Summary

Orexin is a brain chemical that keeps you alert and helps regulate energy use, sleep and hormones. This review argues that faulty orexin signalling, together with disturbances in a gut-brain hormone called GLP-1, may help explain the exhaustion, sleep problems and metabolic changes seen in long COVID and ME/CFS.

## Why this matters

It points to two hormone systems that already have approved drugs — orexin-targeting medicines and GLP-1 receptor agonists used for diabetes and weight loss — as possible starting points for treating the underlying biology of ME/CFS and long COVID, not just the symptoms.
