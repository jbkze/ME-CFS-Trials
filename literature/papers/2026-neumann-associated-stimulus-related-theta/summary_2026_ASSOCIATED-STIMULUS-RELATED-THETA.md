## Summary

In a large German post-viral cohort, brainwave recordings (EEG) showed that people who felt more fatigued had different patterns of brain activity (theta and alpha waves) during mental tasks, even when their measured performance looked normal.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Offers an objective, brain-based marker for the mismatch between how fatigued someone feels and how they perform on tests — a mismatch that is common and hard to explain in ME/CFS and long COVID.

## Notes

German NAPKON/COVIDOM cohort (Kiel-based group; Neumann, Welzel, Keil, Schreiber, Vehreschild JJ among authors), n=98 with n=2,549 replication. Not Wirth/Scheibenbogen-affiliated. Confirmed via Europe PMC REST API record (title/authors/journal/date/DOI exact match).
