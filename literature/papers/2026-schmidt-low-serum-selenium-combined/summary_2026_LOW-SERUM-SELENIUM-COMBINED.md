## Summary

Selenium is a mineral the body needs, and SELENOP is a blood protein that carries it around. In about 750 adults roughly two years after a COVID-19 infection, people with low selenium levels and antibodies that mistakenly attack SELENOP were more likely to still have fatigue — hinting that a mineral shortage, possibly made worse by the immune system attacking its carrier, might contribute to long-lasting tiredness.

## Why this matters

Points to a new, easily measurable biomarker (blood selenium plus SELENOP-autoantibodies) for post-viral fatigue and raises an obvious next question — whether selenium supplementation could help — that could be tested in a trial.
