## Summary

Brain scans of people with long COVID showed fewer working dopamine-releasing nerve endings in a brain region involved in motivation and movement, and the loss lined up with how apathetic, slowed-down, or forgetful people felt.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Points to a specific, measurable brain change behind some long COVID motivation and thinking problems, and suggests boosting dopamine signalling could be worth testing as a treatment.
