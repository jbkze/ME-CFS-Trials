## Summary

Researchers compared 40 teenagers (10–17) with ME/CFS to 595 adults with the illness. Most teens missed school, and many had home schooling. Crashes after activity, unrefreshing sleep, fatigue and thinking problems were common, and many symptoms looked much like those of adults.

## Why this matters

It shows ME/CFS in young people is just as disruptive as in adults, and supports spotting it early and giving school and medical support. The teen group is small.
