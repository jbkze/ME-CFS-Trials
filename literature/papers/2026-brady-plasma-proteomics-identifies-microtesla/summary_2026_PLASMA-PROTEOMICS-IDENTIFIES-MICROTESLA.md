## Summary

In a small trial of a gentle, at-home, whole-brain magnetic-field treatment ("Microtesla Magnetic Therapy") for long-COVID brain fog, some treated patients' thinking and symptoms improved. Researchers then measured over 11,000 blood proteins before and after treatment and found a pattern of about 17 proteins that changed specifically in people who got better, including one protein that normally puts the brakes on a cell-signalling pathway (called Hedgehog signalling) tied to inflammation and tissue repair — suggesting the treatment may work partly by calming inflammation.

## Why this matters

Offers early molecular evidence that a non-drug, brain-stimulation approach can produce a measurable biological change in long COVID, and flags specific blood proteins that could help predict who will respond to this or similar treatments — though this is a small feasibility study, not a definitive trial.
