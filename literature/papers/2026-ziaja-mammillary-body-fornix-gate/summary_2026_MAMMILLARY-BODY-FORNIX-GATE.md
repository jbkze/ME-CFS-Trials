## Summary

Brain scans (structural and diffusion MRI, which maps the brain's internal wiring) compared 88 long-COVID patients who had unusual symptoms — tremors, internal 'vibration' feelings, and severe fatigue — against 34 healthy people. The patients showed differences in a small, deep brain structure pair called the mammillary bodies (involved in memory and relaying body signals) and in the nerve-fibre tracts connecting them, plus changes in the cerebellum (the brain's movement-coordination centre).

## Why this matters

Proposes a specific, physical brain-wiring explanation for the unusual tremor, internal-vibration and fatigue symptoms some long-COVID and ME/CFS patients report, giving researchers a concrete brain region to study further — but it's an early, small, not-yet-peer-reviewed exploratory study.

## Notes

Posted medRxiv 2026-08-03.
