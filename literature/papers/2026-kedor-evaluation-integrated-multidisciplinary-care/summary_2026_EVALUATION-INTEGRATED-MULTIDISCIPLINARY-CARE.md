# Evaluation of an Integrated Multidisciplinary Care Model for Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome: A Prospective, Open-label, Non-randomized Controlled Intervention Study

## Summary

Charité researchers compared two ways of caring for ME/CFS patients over a year: one group got an intensive multi-part program (specialist assessment plus inpatient rehab), the other just a single outpatient visit. Physical-functioning scores ended up statistically the same in both groups, and on a disability scale nearly half of the intensively-treated group (42 of 94) actually got worse, versus only 14% who improved.

## Why this matters

This rigorous, real-world test from Scheibenbogen's own center found that today's rehabilitation-based care does not change the course of ME/CFS, reinforcing her group's broader argument that patients need disease-modifying drug treatments, not more rehab programs.
