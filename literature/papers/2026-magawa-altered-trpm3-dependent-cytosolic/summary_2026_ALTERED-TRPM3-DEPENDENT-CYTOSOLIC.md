# Altered TRPM3-Dependent Cytosolic and Mitochondrial Calcium Influx in Natural Killer Cells of Post-COVID-19 Condition Patients

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-magawa-altered-trpm3-dependent-cytosolic/citation.bib]]

## Summary

In a small study (8 long-COVID patients, 8 healthy people), a faulty calcium channel called TRPM3 — a gateway that lets calcium into cells — let less calcium into the main body of immune cells called natural killer cells, but let too much build up inside the cells' energy-making mitochondria, compared with healthy cells.

## Why this matters

The same TRPM3 channel fault has already been linked to ME/CFS in larger studies from this research group, so finding it in long COVID too strengthens the case for a shared underlying cause and a possible drug target — though this study is very small (8 vs. 8) and needs replication.
