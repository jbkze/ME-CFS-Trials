## Summary

Medical University of Vienna researchers tested blood from 206 long-COVID patients and 71 healthy people, looking at ratios of tiny genetic messenger molecules (microRNAs) linked to mitochondrial and blood-vessel health. Two of these ratios were reliably lower in long-COVID patients in two separate patient groups, though the test's accuracy dropped when checked against an outside dataset.

## Why this matters

A step toward an objective blood test for long COVID that could plausibly extend to ME/CFS diagnosis, though the authors are upfront that it isn't accurate enough yet for clinical use.
