## Summary

An Austrian team worked with patients and experts through several rounds of feedback to build a new 13-question survey that specifically measures post-exertional malaise (PEM) — the 'crash' after activity that is the hallmark symptom of ME/CFS.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

A patient-informed, purpose-built way to measure PEM could improve diagnosis and become a standard outcome measure in future ME/CFS drug trials, though full scientific validation is still pending.
