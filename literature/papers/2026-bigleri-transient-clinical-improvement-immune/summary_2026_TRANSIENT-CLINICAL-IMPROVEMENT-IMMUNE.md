# Transient clinical improvement and immune modulation following recombinant Zoster vaccination in a patient with ME/CFS

## Summary

A woman in her 60s with severe, long-standing ME/CFS felt much better for a short time after each shingles vaccine dose, with better sleep, thinking, pain and movement, before relapsing. Blood tests showed some immune cells working better for a while.

## Why this matters

It is a single case, so it proves nothing, but it hints that giving the immune system a jolt can briefly ease symptoms, which may help researchers understand what is stuck.

## Notes

Single-patient case report with flow cytometry and galectin-9 measurements; not evidence of treatment effect.
