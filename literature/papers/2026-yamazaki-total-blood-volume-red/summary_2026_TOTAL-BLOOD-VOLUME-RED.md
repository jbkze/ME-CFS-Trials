# Total blood volume and red blood cell volume are not associated with orthostatic intolerance in adults with myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome

## Summary

Researchers measured the actual amount of blood and red blood cells in 49 adults with ME/CFS, then tested how their bodies reacted to standing up (orthostatic intolerance — dizziness, a racing heart, or breathing changes when upright). More than half had an abnormal standing-test result, but whether someone had low, normal, or high blood volume made no difference to who was affected.

## Why this matters

Low blood volume has long been suspected as a cause of standing intolerance in ME/CFS; this study suggests it isn't the main driver, pointing instead to nerves, blood vessels, breathing, or brain blood flow as more promising targets for treatment.
