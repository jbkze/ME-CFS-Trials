# Multiomic and Spatial Profiling of Colorectal Tissue Reveals Viral Persistence and Immune Dysregulation in Long COVID

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-lafranchi-multiomic-spatial-profiling-colorectal/citation.bib]]

## Summary

In gut-tissue samples and blood from 44 people with long COVID (compared to 13 who fully recovered), scientists found leftover pieces of the coronavirus still sitting in the gut lining. Nearby immune cells were both driving ongoing inflammation there and, oddly, failing to mount the response needed to fully clear the virus.

## Why this matters

Strengthens the 'viral persistence' theory — that leftover fragments of the original infection can keep the immune system in a low-grade fight — a leading explanation also considered for ME/CFS and a rationale for antiviral or immune-targeted treatment trials.
