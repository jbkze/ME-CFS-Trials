## Summary

In 1,673 people with post-COVID condition in Germany, researchers grouped patients by their pattern of 14 symptoms (including fatigue) rather than by a single label, and found distinct subgroups that differed in age, sex, and psychological factors.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

A large German post-COVID cohort adds to the case that "post-COVID condition" isn't one single illness but several patient subgroups, which matters for tailoring treatment and for how future German trials define who to enroll.
