## Summary

Pooling 22 earlier studies, people with ME/CFS were found to be about seven times more likely to also have irritable bowel syndrome (IBS, a common gut condition) than people without ME/CFS — roughly 1 in 3 ME/CFS patients also has IBS.

## Why this matters

This strong, consistent overlap supports shared underlying biology (immune, nerve, and gut-signalling problems) and strengthens the case for gut-brain-axis-targeted research and care rather than treating fatigue and gut symptoms separately.

## Notes

PROSPERO-registered systematic review + meta-analysis (22 studies reviewed, 18 pooled). Not Wirth/Scheibenbogen-affiliated; complements already-tracked gut-microbiome/tryptophan and genetic-overlap (ME/CFS-IBS) papers. Confirmed via Europe PMC REST API.
