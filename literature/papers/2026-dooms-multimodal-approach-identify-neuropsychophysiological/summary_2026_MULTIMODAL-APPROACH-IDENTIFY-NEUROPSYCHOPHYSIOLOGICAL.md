## Summary

This is a study protocol (a research plan, not results yet) describing a planned Belgian study that will test 115 ME/CFS patients and 55 healthy people with a wide mix of measurements — inflammation markers, brain scans for inflammation, nervous-system function, gut bacteria, and stress-hormone responses — then follow patients through a rehabilitation programme to see which measurements predict who improves.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

Aims to sort ME/CFS patients into biological subgroups so future treatment (including rehabilitation) can be matched to what's actually driving each patient's illness, rather than one-size-fits-all — worth watching for when results are published.
