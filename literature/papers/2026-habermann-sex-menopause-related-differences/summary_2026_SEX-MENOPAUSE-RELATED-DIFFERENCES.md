## Summary

In survey data from 748 German ME/CFS patients, the authors found that immune-type symptoms (like sore throat or swollen glands) and gut symptoms group together differently in women than in men — more separate in women, more tangled together in men. Going through menopause changed women's immune symptoms but not their gut symptoms.

## Why this matters

A direct follow-up on the same German patient cohort already tracked here, adding evidence that ME/CFS may need to be split into sex- and menopause-specific subgroups — relevant to designing better-targeted future drug trials.
