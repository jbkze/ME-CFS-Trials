# Global and local genetic overlap among ME/CFS, irritable bowel syndrome and psychiatric traits: a hypothesis-generating analysis

## Related files
- [[literature/papers/2026-lee-global-local-genetic-overlap/citation.bib]]

## Summary

This genetic study compared ME/CFS with irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), depression, and loneliness using large genetic databases. Overall, ME/CFS shared a lot of genetic background with IBS and depression, but when the analysis zoomed in on specific spots in the genome, very little of that overlap held up — and the genes linked to ME/CFS risk were concentrated in nerve cells (including gut nerve cells), not in depression-related brain circuits.

## Why this matters

Even though ME/CFS, IBS and depression often occur together and can look similar, this suggests ME/CFS has its own distinct genetic roots in the nervous system rather than simply being a form of depression — relevant to arguments that ME/CFS deserves biomedical treatment research, not just psychiatric care.
