## Summary

Using wrist-worn fitness trackers on almost 1,500 people an average of about 21 months after COVID-19, researchers found that those with a high long-COVID symptom burden had lower heart-rate variability at rest, a faster resting heart rate, and were far less physically active day-to-day than those with a low symptom burden — showing the fitness drop is real, measurable, and does not simply fade with time.

## Why this matters

Objectively confirms, with wearable data rather than just questionnaires, that long COVID can cause a lasting drop in heart and activity fitness nearly two years out, and raises the question of whether long COVID itself becomes a long-term heart-health risk factor.
