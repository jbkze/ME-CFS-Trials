## Summary

Using a more precise blood test than earlier studies, researchers re-measured common inflammation markers (CRP, TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6) in long-COVID patients recruited from a Spanish primary-care clinic, and found no evidence these markers were raised compared with recovered or healthy people.

_(Full text in `paper.txt`; the summary above is the curated plain-language abstract — deepen from the full text when reading.)_

## Why this matters

A negative finding that argues against simple blood-inflammation markers as a reliable way to diagnose or explain long COVID, pushing the search for a cause toward other biology (such as microclots or immune-cell dysfunction) instead.
