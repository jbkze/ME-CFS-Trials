## Summary

Reviewing 142 studies on ME/CFS and long-COVID together, the authors found a signalling pathway called NF-κB (a switch that turns on inflammation) keeps showing up as a common thread linking many different lab findings, alongside shared disruptions in amino-acid, energy, fat, and gut-bacteria processing.

## Why this matters

A large attempt to organise the scattered ME/CFS/long-COVID biomarker literature into one framework, useful for future diagnostic-test and drug-target research — from a German group (Hannover Medical School), though not Charité-affiliated.
