## Summary

This review argues that dizziness and fatigue on standing in ME/CFS often comes from two under-recognized causes — an unusually small heart pumping less blood, and a subtle balance-system (inner-ear/brain) problem — not just blood pooling in the legs.

## Why this matters

Proposes specific, testable physical causes for a core disabling symptom, pointing toward different treatment targets (heart size/output, the balance system) than usual orthostatic-intolerance care.
