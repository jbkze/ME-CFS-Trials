# Myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome and overlapping post-acute infection syndromes: international expert consensus framework for diagnostic standardization

## Summary

19 international ME/CFS experts agreed on a standard way to measure and score seven core symptoms of ME/CFS and related post-infection illnesses (including long COVID) — such as post-exertional malaise (a crash after activity), autonomic problems (trouble with automatic body functions like heart rate or blood pressure), brain fog, and sleep issues — plus a tiered testing plan that avoids unnecessary or risky tests.

## Why this matters

A shared, validated way to measure ME/CFS symptoms makes future studies and drug trials easier to compare across different research centers, which is a practical building block for better trial design.
