# The efficacy of exercise in patients with myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome: A systematic review and meta-analysis

## Related files

## Summary

Combining results from 17 controlled trials with almost 2,000 ME/CFS patients, exercise programmes improved fatigue, sleep, and quality of life, but did not meaningfully improve heart/lung fitness or physical capacity.

## Why this matters

Exercise therapy is a historically controversial topic in ME/CFS because overexertion can trigger post-exertional malaise (a crash after activity). This large pooled analysis suggests exercise may ease some symptoms but is not a fix for the underlying physical limitation, supporting a cautious, symptom-management role rather than a cure.
