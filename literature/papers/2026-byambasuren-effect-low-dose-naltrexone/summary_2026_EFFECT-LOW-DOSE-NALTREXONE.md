## Summary

This review pooled the available studies on low-dose naltrexone (LDN) — a cheap generic drug widely used off-label by long-COVID and ME/CFS patients — to see whether it actually helps with symptoms like fatigue.

## Why this matters

LDN is one of the most commonly self-tried treatments in this patient community, so an independent pooled review of the evidence directly informs whether it deserves a proper, dedicated clinical trial.
