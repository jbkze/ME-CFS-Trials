## Summary

Researchers built a new computer method to reliably combine several small, messy studies of gene activity in long COVID and found a consistent pattern of seven genes, involved in immune response and energy production, that showed up across different patient groups. They then matched these genes to existing drugs that might be repurposed to treat long COVID.

## Why this matters

Gives researchers a shortlist of biological targets and already-approved drugs worth investigating for long COVID, and a reusable method for finding similar signals in related conditions like ME/CFS.
