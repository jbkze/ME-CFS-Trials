## Summary

In this German trial (Mainz), 40 people with long-term post-COVID symptoms and high levels of self-attacking antibodies had those antibodies filtered out of their blood (immunoadsorption), compared with a fake (sham) version of the same procedure. The real treatment did remove the antibodies as intended, but patients did not feel any better than those who got the sham treatment, and side effects were more common with the real treatment.

## Why this matters

This is a rigorous, placebo-controlled negative result for a treatment approach (removing 'self-attacking' antibodies) that other German trials — including Scheibenbogen's — are also testing in post-COVID/ME-CFS, so it's an important caution: filtering out these antibodies doesn't automatically translate into symptom relief, at least in this dosing/design.
