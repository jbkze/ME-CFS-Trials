## Summary

This paper proposes a new explanation for why people crash one to three days after activity in long COVID (post-exertional malaise, PEM): damaged mitochondria (the cell's energy factories) get flagged for clean-up, but the clean-up process doesn't finish properly, so leftover mitochondrial debris keeps triggering inflammation long after the activity itself is over.

## Why this matters

A plausible new piece for the PEM puzzle — mitochondrial 'clean-up failure' rather than simply low energy — that could in principle be tested with existing lab methods, though it is one author's hypothesis with no new experimental data behind it yet.
