## Summary

Using a special camera that watches how the tiny blood vessels at the back of the eye react to a flickering light, researchers compared 96 healthy people, 102 people who recovered fully from COVID-19, and 102 people with lasting post-COVID symptoms (62 of whom met full ME/CFS criteria). The vessels of post-COVID patients widened less in response to the flicker and were narrower at rest, and this problem was worst in the ME/CFS group, whose blood also showed higher levels of markers linked to inflamed, damaged blood-vessel walls.

## Why this matters

Offers a simple, non-invasive eye test as a way to spot and measure the blood-vessel problems thought to underlie ME/CFS, which could help diagnose patients earlier and track whether future vascular-targeted treatments are actually working.
